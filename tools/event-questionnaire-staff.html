STEP 8Beverages

Alcohol-free: no alcohol will be served at your event, and guests may not bring their own. This becomes part of your event contract. Non-alcoholic beverages are still available below.

Every bar package has a $75 setup fee and $15 per bartender per hour, with one bartender plus one more for every 30 guests. Leave the bar blank if you don't need one.

  • Open bar, beer and wine. $16 per guest for the first hour, $12 per guest for each hour after.
  • Open bar, beer, wine and cocktails. $18 per guest for the first hour, $16 per guest for each hour after.
    Classic cocktails: Cosmopolitan (Albany Distilling Vodka), Martini and Negroni (Deep Wells Gin), Old Fashioned (Fort Hamilton Whiskey), Manhattan (Fort Hamilton Rye), Margarita (Steelbound Agave), Daiquiri (Albany Distilling Rum).
  • Cash bar. You cover the setup and bartender fees; each guest pays their own bar tab at the end of the meal.
  • Consumption bar. You cover the setup and bartender fees, and the balance of the bar tab is due from you at the end of the meal.
