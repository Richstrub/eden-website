About Us

Eden is a wood-fired restaurant in a restored historic building on Hanover Square in downtown Syracuse. Everything we serve is cooked over an open hearth at the center of our kitchen, fueled by wood and guided by the seasons.

Our mission is to highlight the best farmers, foragers, brewers, distillers and food producers in New York State. Our menu changes with each harvest, and every dish names the farm or producer it comes from, so you know exactly where your dinner began.

The bar pours New York State spirits, craft cocktails and a wine list chosen with the same care as the food.

Eden is a Wine Spectator Restaurant Award winner, a DiRōNA Award of Excellence recipient, and in 2026 we hosted our first Friends of James Beard Benefit dinner.

Fire. Farm. Food. Wine.

Our Partners

Soil

Grindstone Farm

Pulaski, NY

Richard de Graff and his wife, Vic, have farmed in Pulaski for 45 years, 38 of them certified organic, with Anna Joy Corson as their lead gardener. Midway between Syracuse and Watertown, they grow organic vegetables, berries, herbs and tree fruit, and sell through their CSA, an on-farm market and farmers markets across Central New York.

Their story & map Website Facebook Instagram

Fresh Herbs of Fabius

Fabius, NY · (315) 683-5159

Producers of gourmet vegetables, edible flowers, and microgreens since 2000, delivering to all points between Utica and Auburn. Microgreens and flowers available all year.

Their story & map Facebook Instagram

Fruit of the Fungi

Lebanon, NY

KC and Kristi Mangine began growing shiitake on logs in 2008 and went full time in 2015. Today they grow certified organic shiitake, lion's mane, oyster, maitake, beech, chestnut and reishi mushrooms, all on wood using sawdust from local mills. Find them year-round at the CNY Regional Market's Saturday farmers market.

Their story & map

Dutch Hill Maple

Tully, NY

Making maple syrup in the hills of southern Onondaga County since 1968, Dutch Hill is a family operation now led by Nate and Cristy Williams; Nate grew up sugaring with his father and brother. From their new sugarhouse on Woodmancy Road they produce pure and aged maple syrup, maple cream and maple sugar, and are NYS Grown & Certified. Visit during New York's Maple Weekends to taste it at the source.

Their story & map Website Facebook Instagram
Pasture

Hillside Farm

Truxton, NY

Alexa and Jacob Newton raise beef, pork, chickens and turkeys in the hills of Cortland County. Their birds live in shelters that move across the pasture so they can graze and forage outdoors, and the Newtons process their poultry right on the farm.

Their story & map Facebook Instagram

Sorrell Hill Milk Co.

Baldwinsville, NY

Sara and Josh Kerrick met showing cows at the Cayuga County Fair. In 2025 they opened Sorrell Hill Milk Co., an on-farm creamery at 2 Kids Goat Farm. They're home to the CregoCrest Registered Holsteins and make creamline milk and fresh cheese from both cow and goat milk, with every animal in the small herd known by name.

Their story & map Website Facebook Instagram

Certified Angus Beef

Wooster, OH

Founded in 1978 by ranchers working with an Ohio State University meat scientist, the Certified Angus Beef brand judges every cut against 10 quality standards. It is the source of the Ember Roasted NY Strip we cook over the hearth.

Their story & map Website Facebook Instagram
Sea

Browne Trading Co.

Portland, ME

Committed to the purchase and supply of responsibly sourced seafood, meeting customers' needs without compromising the future health of our oceans' fish.

Their story & map Website Facebook Instagram

Wester Ross Salmon

Ullapool, Scotland

Founded in 1977 near Ullapool, Wester Ross is one of the oldest salmon farms in Scotland. Its Atlantic salmon are fed and tended by hand in small pens in the tidal sea lochs of the northwest Highlands: Loch Kanaird, Loch Broom and Little Loch Broom.

Their story & map Website Facebook Instagram
Vine

Dr. Konstantin Frank Winery

Hammondsport, NY

Konstantin Frank Winery was founded on Keuka Lake in 1962 by a Ukrainian-born viticulturist who insisted, against the conventional wisdom of the day, that European vinifera grapes could survive Finger Lakes winters. Four generations on, the family has kept pushing into new varieties. In 2006 Frank’s grandson Fred bought land on the east side of Seneca Lake in Hector and began planting Grüner Veltliner, Austria’s signature white grape, building what is now one of the largest plantings of it in the United States.

Their story & map Website

Hermann J. Wiemer Vineyard

Dundee, NY

Wiemer grew up in Bernkastel on Germany’s Mosel, where his father ran a vine-grafting research station and taught him to graft Riesling onto American rootstock. He came to New York in 1965, saw the Mosel in Seneca Lake’s steep slopes and cool climate, and in 1973 bought a farm on the lake’s west shore near Dundee. His winery opened in 1979 and became one of the pioneers of Finger Lakes Riesling. Since 2007 it has been run by his former apprentice Fred Merwarth and partner Oskar Bynke.

Their story & map Website

Heart & Hands Wine Company

Union Springs, NY

Heart & Hands is the Cayuga Lake winery of Tom and Susan Higgins, who went looking for limestone before they went looking for land. Tom, a Finger Lakes native, left a technology career for wine, working at Zachy’s, at Château La Lagune in Bordeaux and in local cellars. In 2007 the couple bought a gentle slope above Carr’s Cove on the lake’s eastern shore, just north of Union Springs, and planted their first vines in 2010. Today they make about 2,000 cases a year of cool-climate wines.

Their story & map Website

Living Roots Wine & Co.

Hammondsport, NY

Living Roots Wine & Co. is the winery of Sebastian and Colleen Hardy, founded in 2016 and split between the Finger Lakes and South Australia. Seb is a sixth-generation winemaker: his ancestor Thomas Hardy arrived in South Australia in 1850 and planted some of its first vines, and his father, Geoff Hardy, planted K1, the first vineyard in Kuitpo, a cool, high subregion at the southern end of the Adelaide Hills. Colleen, from the Rochester area, met Seb while working a vintage in Australia. In 2023 Halliday’s Wine Companion named Living Roots Australia’s Best New Winery.

Their story & map

Grace Tyler Estate Winery

Constantia, NY

Grace Tyler Estate Winery is Richard and Elizabeth Hamilton’s farm winery on the north shore of Oneida Lake in Constantia. They bought the land in 2012, harvested their first grapes in 2017 and opened in 2018. Their tasting room is built from aspen cut on the site, it is open year-round and can be reached by snowmobile trail. They grow and work with grapes that can survive Oswego County winters.

Their story & map
Still

Lock 1 Distilling Company

Phoenix, NY

Lock 1 Distilling Company is a family farm distillery in Phoenix, the Oswego County village where the Oswego Canal begins its run north from the Erie Canal toward Lake Ontario. Cousins Brenden Backus, Stephen Dates and Kevin Dates opened it in 2015 across from the canal’s Lock 1, and built most of their equipment themselves in Backus’s fabrication shop.

Their story & map Website

Old Home Distillers

Lebanon, NY

Old Home Distillers is a family farm distillery on historic Campbell Road in Lebanon, Madison County, southeast of Syracuse. Jerry and Marlene Carvell founded it in 2015 in a hundred-year-old stable they converted into a stillhouse, and their sons Aaron and Adam help run it. Everything is made on the farm from New York-grown ingredients, from the corn whiskey that started the business to the bourbons, ryes and gins that followed.

Their story & map

Hollerhorn Distilling

Naples, NY

Hollerhorn Distilling is Karl and Melissa Neubauer’s family operation in the hills outside Naples, at the head of Canandaigua Lake, opened in 2018 and rebuilt after a fire in 2022. Karl makes every spirit himself, drawing on years of study with master distillers and a family tradition of Austrian schnapps.

Their story & map Website

Fort Hamilton Distillery

Brooklyn, NY

Fort Hamilton Distillery was founded in 2016 by Alex Clark, a British-born bartender who spent years in New York’s classic-cocktail scene, and his wife, Amy Grindeland. Their mission was to bring back New York’s earliest whiskey style, rye, and the distillery takes its name from Fort Hamilton at the southern tip of Brooklyn, itself named for Alexander Hamilton. Since 2020 the company has had its own distillery and tasting room on the second floor of a converted warehouse in Industry City, on the Sunset Park waterfront.

Their story & map

Albany Distilling Company

Albany, NY

Albany Distilling Company calls itself the first licensed distillery in Albany since Prohibition. Teachers John Curtin and Matthew Jager opened it in 2011 in a small space on Montgomery Street, beside the Albany Pump Station and close to where the Quackenbush Still House, the city’s first distillery, operated in the mid-1700s. Curtin and co-owner Rick Sicari have since grown it into a much larger production house on Third Street, with a bar and bottle shop downtown.

Their story & map

Kitchen Literacy Project

Syracuse, NY

Founded in 2023 by Jess Miller, the Kitchen Literacy Project teaches Syracuse teens to cook, free of charge, at Salt City Market. Local chefs teach paid cooking classes for the public, and that money pays for the free teen program, giving young people kitchen skills they can use for life.
